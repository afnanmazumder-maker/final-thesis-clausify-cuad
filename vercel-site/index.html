What it does

A contract goes in. The system detects which of CUAD's 41 clause categories it contains, extracts the clause text, attaches a risk level from the weaker party's point of view, and sorts the findings so the clauses worth worrying about appear first.

Honest limits

The risk badge and category name are more reliable than the quoted clause text beneath them. End-to-end clause survival is 21.7%, the confidence bar is not calibrated (ECE 0.201), and the risk levels were assigned by three CS students and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Read a card as "this contract appears to contain a clause of this type, look here", not as "this is the clause". This is a research prototype, not legal advice.

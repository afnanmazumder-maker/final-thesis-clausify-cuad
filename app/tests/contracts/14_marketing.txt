CO-MARKETING AND BRAND PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT



This Co-Marketing And Brand Partnership Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Solstice Media Holdings, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Solstice"), and Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co., with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Fernwood"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Solstice Media Holdings owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co. desires to obtain, and Solstice Media Holdings is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. REVENUE SHARE. Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co. shall pay to Solstice Media Holdings fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

2. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co. shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Solstice Media Holdings, and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Solstice Media Holdings.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Neither Party shall make, publish or communicate any statement, whether written or oral, that disparages or is reasonably likely to harm the reputation, goodwill or commercial interests of the other Party, its officers, directors or products.

5. NON-SOLICITATION OF CUSTOMERS. During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co. shall not solicit, divert or attempt to divert the business of any customer or prospective customer of Solstice Media Holdings with whom Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co. had material contact during the Term.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Solstice Media Holdings may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Fernwood Outdoor Gear Co., without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

8. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Colorado, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

11. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

12. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.

13. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

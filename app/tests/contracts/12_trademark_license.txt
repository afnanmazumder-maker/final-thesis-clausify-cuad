TRADEMARK LICENSE AGREEMENT



This Trademark License Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Marchetti Brands S.p.A., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Marchetti"), and Lakeshore Apparel Partners, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Lakeshore"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Marchetti Brands S.p.A. owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Lakeshore Apparel Partners desires to obtain, and Marchetti Brands S.p.A. is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. GRANT OF LICENSE. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Marchetti Brands S.p.A. grants to Lakeshore Apparel Partners a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Software in object code form solely for Lakeshore Apparel Partners's internal business purposes during the Term.

2. NON-TRANSFERABILITY. The license granted hereunder is personal to Lakeshore Apparel Partners and may not be assigned, sublicensed, rented, leased or otherwise transferred to any third party, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. AFFILIATE RIGHTS. The rights granted to Lakeshore Apparel Partners under this Agreement shall extend to each of Lakeshore Apparel Partners's Affiliates, provided that Lakeshore Apparel Partners remains responsible for each such Affiliate's compliance with the terms hereof.

5. LICENSOR AFFILIATES. The license granted herein is made by Marchetti Brands S.p.A. on behalf of itself and each of its Affiliates, and Marchetti Brands S.p.A. represents that it has authority to bind such Affiliates to the grant.

6. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

7. REVENUE SHARE. Lakeshore Apparel Partners shall pay to Marchetti Brands S.p.A. fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

8. PRICING. Lakeshore Apparel Partners shall not offer the Products at a resale price below the minimum advertised price published by Marchetti Brands S.p.A. from time to time, and shall not increase prices to end customers by more than fifteen percent (15%) in any twelve (12) month period without prior written approval.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10. AUDIT. Marchetti Brands S.p.A. may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Lakeshore Apparel Partners relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Lakeshore Apparel Partners shall bear the cost of the audit.

11. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Neither Party shall make, publish or communicate any statement, whether written or oral, that disparages or is reasonably likely to harm the reputation, goodwill or commercial interests of the other Party, its officers, directors or products.

12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

13. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

14. FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, or failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

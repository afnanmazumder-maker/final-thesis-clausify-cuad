AUTHORIZED RESELLER AGREEMENT



This Authorized Reseller Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Vantage Instruments LLC, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Vantage"), and Kestrel Trading Company, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Kestrel"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Vantage Instruments LLC owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Kestrel Trading Company desires to obtain, and Vantage Instruments LLC is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Kestrel Trading Company shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Vantage Instruments LLC, and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Vantage Instruments LLC.

2. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Kestrel Trading Company shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Kestrel Trading Company at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. MOST FAVORED CUSTOMER. Kestrel Trading Company shall extend to Vantage Instruments LLC pricing, terms and conditions no less favorable than the most favorable pricing, terms and conditions Kestrel Trading Company extends to any other vendor or customer for comparable goods or services, and shall promptly notify Vantage Instruments LLC if more favorable terms are granted to a third party.

5. PRICING. Kestrel Trading Company shall not offer the Products at a resale price below the minimum advertised price published by Vantage Instruments LLC from time to time, and shall not increase prices to end customers by more than fifteen percent (15%) in any twelve (12) month period without prior written approval.

6. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.

7. VOLUME LIMITS. Kestrel Trading Company shall not sell, distribute or transfer more than twelve thousand (12,000) units in any Contract Year without the prior written consent of Vantage Instruments LLC, and shall not aggregate orders across Affiliates to exceed such limit.

8. REVENUE SHARE. Kestrel Trading Company shall pay to Vantage Instruments LLC fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

10. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL. Before offering the Products or any substantially similar opportunity to any third party, Kestrel Trading Company shall first offer the same to Vantage Instruments LLC on terms no less favorable, and Vantage Instruments LLC shall have twenty (20) business days in which to accept such offer.

11. AUDIT. Vantage Instruments LLC may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Kestrel Trading Company relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Kestrel Trading Company shall bear the cost of the audit.

12. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

13. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

14. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

15. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AND CONSULTING AGREEMENT



This Professional Services And Consulting Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Lindhurst Advisory Group, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Lindhurst"), and Orbit Freight Systems, Inc., with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Orbit"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lindhurst Advisory Group owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Orbit Freight Systems, Inc. desires to obtain, and Lindhurst Advisory Group is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Orbit Freight Systems, Inc. hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Lindhurst Advisory Group all right, title and interest in and to any inventions, improvements, derivative works, modifications, documentation and feedback conceived or developed in connection with this Agreement, together with all patent, copyright, trade secret and moral rights therein, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

2. UNLIMITED LIABILITY. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Orbit Freight Systems, Inc.'s liability arising out of its indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or gross negligence shall be unlimited and shall not be subject to any cap, exclusion or limitation set out elsewhere herein.

3. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

4. TRANSITION SERVICES. Following termination or expiration of this Agreement, Orbit Freight Systems, Inc. shall, at Lindhurst Advisory Group's request, continue to provide the Services for a transition period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days on the terms then in effect, and shall cooperate in the orderly migration of data and accounts.

5. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Lindhurst Advisory Group may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Orbit Freight Systems, Inc., without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

7. INSURANCE. Orbit Freight Systems, Inc. shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Lindhurst Advisory Group as an additional insured.

8. WARRANTY. Lindhurst Advisory Group warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

11. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

12. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

14. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

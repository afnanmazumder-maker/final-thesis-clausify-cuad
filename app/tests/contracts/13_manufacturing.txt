CONTRACT MANUFACTURING AGREEMENT



This Contract Manufacturing Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Dunmore Precision Works, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Dunmore"), and Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc., with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Halcyon"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Dunmore Precision Works owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. desires to obtain, and Dunmore Precision Works is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

2. VOLUME LIMITS. Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. shall not sell, distribute or transfer more than twelve thousand (12,000) units in any Contract Year without the prior written consent of Dunmore Precision Works, and shall not aggregate orders across Affiliates to exceed such limit.

3. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

4. WARRANTY. Dunmore Precision Works warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

5. INSURANCE. Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Dunmore Precision Works as an additional insured.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

7. ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Dunmore Precision Works all right, title and interest in and to any inventions, improvements, derivative works, modifications, documentation and feedback conceived or developed in connection with this Agreement, together with all patent, copyright, trade secret and moral rights therein, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

8. AUDIT. Dunmore Precision Works may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc. shall bear the cost of the audit.

9. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

10. CHANGE OF CONTROL. In the event of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets or voting securities of Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc., or any transaction resulting in a change in control of Halcyon Medical Devices, Inc., Dunmore Precision Works may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

11. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Minnesota, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

13. EXPIRATION. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, this Agreement shall expire on 31 December 2027.

14. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.

15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

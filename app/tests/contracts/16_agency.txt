SALES AGENCY AND REPRESENTATION AGREEMENT



This Sales Agency And Representation Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Thornefield Capital Equipment, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Thornefield"), and Delacroix Agencies SARL, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Delacroix"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Thornefield Capital Equipment owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Delacroix Agencies SARL desires to obtain, and Thornefield Capital Equipment is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Delacroix Agencies SARL shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Thornefield Capital Equipment, and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Thornefield Capital Equipment.

2. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Thornefield Capital Equipment may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Delacroix Agencies SARL, without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. COVENANT NOT TO SUE. Delacroix Agencies SARL covenants that it will not assert, and will not authorize any third party to assert on its behalf, any claim, suit or proceeding against Thornefield Capital Equipment or its customers alleging that the Products infringe any patent owned or controlled by Delacroix Agencies SARL as of the Effective Date.

5. NON-SOLICITATION OF CUSTOMERS. During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Delacroix Agencies SARL shall not solicit, divert or attempt to divert the business of any customer or prospective customer of Thornefield Capital Equipment with whom Delacroix Agencies SARL had material contact during the Term.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. REVENUE SHARE. Delacroix Agencies SARL shall pay to Thornefield Capital Equipment fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

8. ASSIGNMENT. Delacroix Agencies SARL shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Thornefield Capital Equipment, and any purported assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

9. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

11. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

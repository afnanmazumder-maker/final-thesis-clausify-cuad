MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT



This Master Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Ironbark Components Ltd., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Ironbark"), and Saltmarsh Appliance Co., with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Saltmarsh"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Ironbark Components Ltd. owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Saltmarsh Appliance Co. desires to obtain, and Ironbark Components Ltd. is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Saltmarsh Appliance Co. shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Saltmarsh Appliance Co. at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

2. MOST FAVORED CUSTOMER. Saltmarsh Appliance Co. shall extend to Ironbark Components Ltd. pricing, terms and conditions no less favorable than the most favorable pricing, terms and conditions Saltmarsh Appliance Co. extends to any other vendor or customer for comparable goods or services, and shall promptly notify Ironbark Components Ltd. if more favorable terms are granted to a third party.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. VOLUME LIMITS. Saltmarsh Appliance Co. shall not sell, distribute or transfer more than twelve thousand (12,000) units in any Contract Year without the prior written consent of Ironbark Components Ltd., and shall not aggregate orders across Affiliates to exceed such limit.

5. CHANGE OF CONTROL. In the event of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets or voting securities of Saltmarsh Appliance Co., or any transaction resulting in a change in control of Saltmarsh Appliance Co., Ironbark Components Ltd. may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. INSURANCE. Saltmarsh Appliance Co. shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Ironbark Components Ltd. as an additional insured.

8. WARRANTY. Ironbark Components Ltd. warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

9. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

11. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Written notice of non-renewal must be given not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term. If such notice is not timely given, this Agreement shall automatically renew and the Parties shall be bound for the full subsequent term.

12. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

13. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

14. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

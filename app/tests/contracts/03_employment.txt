EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT



This Executive Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Caldera Systems Corporation, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Caldera"), and Priya Raghunathan, residing in the State of California ("Raghunathan"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Caldera Systems Corporation wishes to employ Raghunathan in an executive capacity, and Raghunathan wishes to accept such employment, on the terms and conditions set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. NON-COMPETITION. For a period of three (3) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, Priya Raghunathan shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as owner, partner, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage in, invest in, or render services to any business that competes with the products or services of Caldera Systems Corporation within the Territory.

2. NON-SOLICITATION OF PERSONNEL. During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall directly or indirectly solicit for employment, hire or engage any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with this Agreement, excluding responses to general public advertisements.

3. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

4. NON-SOLICITATION OF CUSTOMERS. During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Priya Raghunathan shall not solicit, divert or attempt to divert the business of any customer or prospective customer of Caldera Systems Corporation with whom Priya Raghunathan had material contact during the Term.

5. ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Priya Raghunathan hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Caldera Systems Corporation all right, title and interest in and to any inventions, improvements, derivative works, modifications, documentation and feedback conceived or developed in connection with this Agreement, together with all patent, copyright, trade secret and moral rights therein, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

7. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Neither Party shall make, publish or communicate any statement, whether written or oral, that disparages or is reasonably likely to harm the reputation, goodwill or commercial interests of the other Party, its officers, directors or products.

8. CARVE-OUT. Notwithstanding the restrictions set out in Section 5, nothing herein shall prevent Priya Raghunathan from continuing to sell products in the legacy product lines identified in Schedule C, or from holding a passive investment of less than two percent (2%) in any publicly traded competitor.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

11. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

12. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

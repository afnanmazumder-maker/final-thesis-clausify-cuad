MASTER SOFTWARE LICENSE AND DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT



This Master Software License And Distribution Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Northwind Technologies, Inc., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Northwind"), and Blue Meridian Distributors LLC, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Blue"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Northwind Technologies, Inc. owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Blue Meridian Distributors LLC desires to obtain, and Northwind Technologies, Inc. is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. GRANT OF LICENSE. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Northwind Technologies, Inc. grants to Blue Meridian Distributors LLC a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Software in object code form solely for Blue Meridian Distributors LLC's internal business purposes during the Term.

2. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Northwind Technologies, Inc., and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Northwind Technologies, Inc..

3. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

4. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Blue Meridian Distributors LLC at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

5. MOST FAVORED CUSTOMER. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall extend to Northwind Technologies, Inc. pricing, terms and conditions no less favorable than the most favorable pricing, terms and conditions Blue Meridian Distributors LLC extends to any other vendor or customer for comparable goods or services, and shall promptly notify Northwind Technologies, Inc. if more favorable terms are granted to a third party.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

7. NON-COMPETITION. For a period of three (3) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as owner, partner, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage in, invest in, or render services to any business that competes with the products or services of Northwind Technologies, Inc. within the Territory.

8. ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Northwind Technologies, Inc. all right, title and interest in and to any inventions, improvements, derivative works, modifications, documentation and feedback conceived or developed in connection with this Agreement, together with all patent, copyright, trade secret and moral rights therein, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

9. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10. PERPETUAL LICENSE. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC grants to Northwind Technologies, Inc. an irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up license, with the right to sublicense through multiple tiers, to use, reproduce, modify and distribute any Blue Meridian Distributors LLC materials incorporated into or delivered with the Deliverables.

11. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES. In the event of a breach by Blue Meridian Distributors LLC of its exclusivity or non-competition obligations, Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall pay to Northwind Technologies, Inc. liquidated damages in the amount of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence. The Parties agree that this sum is a reasonable estimate of the damages that would be sustained and does not constitute a penalty.

12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

13. UNLIMITED LIABILITY. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Blue Meridian Distributors LLC's liability arising out of its indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or gross negligence shall be unlimited and shall not be subject to any cap, exclusion or limitation set out elsewhere herein.

14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except as expressly provided otherwise, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

15. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

16. AUDIT. Northwind Technologies, Inc. may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Blue Meridian Distributors LLC relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall bear the cost of the audit.

17. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

18. FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, or failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

19. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Written notice of non-renewal must be given not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term. If such notice is not timely given, this Agreement shall automatically renew and the Parties shall be bound for the full subsequent term.

20. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Northwind Technologies, Inc. may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Blue Meridian Distributors LLC, without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

21. ASSIGNMENT. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Northwind Technologies, Inc., and any purported assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

22. CHANGE OF CONTROL. In the event of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets or voting securities of Blue Meridian Distributors LLC, or any transaction resulting in a change in control of Blue Meridian Distributors LLC, Northwind Technologies, Inc. may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

23. INSURANCE. Blue Meridian Distributors LLC shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Northwind Technologies, Inc. as an additional insured.

24. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

25. EXPIRATION. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, this Agreement shall expire on 31 December 2027.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

JOINT DEVELOPMENT AND COLLABORATION AGREEMENT



This Joint Development And Collaboration Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Alder & Finch Biotech, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Alder"), and Marrow Point Research Institute, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Marrow"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Alder & Finch Biotech owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Marrow Point Research Institute desires to obtain, and Alder & Finch Biotech is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. JOINT OWNERSHIP. Any intellectual property jointly conceived by personnel of both Parties in the course of the Project shall be owned jointly by the Parties, each having an undivided one-half interest, and neither Party shall be required to account to the other for exploitation of such jointly owned property.

2. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Marrow Point Research Institute shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Alder & Finch Biotech, and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Alder & Finch Biotech.

3. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

4. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL. Before offering the Products or any substantially similar opportunity to any third party, Marrow Point Research Institute shall first offer the same to Alder & Finch Biotech on terms no less favorable, and Alder & Finch Biotech shall have twenty (20) business days in which to accept such offer.

5. REVENUE SHARE. Marrow Point Research Institute shall pay to Alder & Finch Biotech fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

7. NON-SOLICITATION OF PERSONNEL. During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall directly or indirectly solicit for employment, hire or engage any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with this Agreement, excluding responses to general public advertisements.

8. TRANSITION SERVICES. Following termination or expiration of this Agreement, Marrow Point Research Institute shall, at Alder & Finch Biotech's request, continue to provide the Services for a transition period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days on the terms then in effect, and shall cooperate in the orderly migration of data and accounts.

9. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

11. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

13. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

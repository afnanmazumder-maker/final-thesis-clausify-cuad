FRANCHISE AGREEMENT



This Franchise Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Copperpot"), and Westbridge Hospitality LLC, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Westbridge"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Westbridge Hospitality LLC desires to obtain, and Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. NON-COMPETITION. For a period of three (3) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as owner, partner, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage in, invest in, or render services to any business that competes with the products or services of Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. within the Territory.

2. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc., and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc..

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Westbridge Hospitality LLC at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

5. REVENUE SHARE. Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall pay to Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. fifteen percent (15%) of all gross revenue recognized from the sale, sublicense or distribution of the Products, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, together with a statement showing the calculation.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. PRICING. Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall not offer the Products at a resale price below the minimum advertised price published by Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. from time to time, and shall not increase prices to end customers by more than fifteen percent (15%) in any twelve (12) month period without prior written approval.

8. AUDIT. Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Westbridge Hospitality LLC relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall bear the cost of the audit.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

10. ASSIGNMENT. Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc., and any purported assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

11. CHANGE OF CONTROL. In the event of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets or voting securities of Westbridge Hospitality LLC, or any transaction resulting in a change in control of Westbridge Hospitality LLC, Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

12. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.

13. INSURANCE. Westbridge Hospitality LLC shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Copperpot Restaurant Group, Inc. as an additional insured.

14. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

15. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

16. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

SOFTWARE SUPPORT AND SOURCE CODE ESCROW AGREEMENT



This Software Support And Source Code Escrow Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Pallas Software Limited, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Pallas"), and Granite Bay Credit Union, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Granite"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Pallas Software Limited owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Granite Bay Credit Union desires to obtain, and Pallas Software Limited is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. SOURCE CODE ESCROW. Pallas Software Limited shall deposit a complete copy of the source code, build scripts and documentation for the Software with a mutually agreed escrow agent within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, and shall update the deposit upon each major release. The escrowed materials shall be released to Granite Bay Credit Union upon the occurrence of a Release Condition, including Pallas Software Limited's insolvency or sustained failure to provide Support.

2. TRANSITION SERVICES. Following termination or expiration of this Agreement, Granite Bay Credit Union shall, at Pallas Software Limited's request, continue to provide the Services for a transition period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days on the terms then in effect, and shall cooperate in the orderly migration of data and accounts.

3. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

4. WARRANTY. Pallas Software Limited warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except as expressly provided otherwise, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

8. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Written notice of non-renewal must be given not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term. If such notice is not timely given, this Agreement shall automatically renew and the Parties shall be bound for the full subsequent term.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10. THIRD PARTY BENEFICIARY. The Parties agree that Centurion Assurance Group shall be an intended third party beneficiary of the warranties and indemnities set out in Sections 8 and 9, and shall be entitled to enforce the same directly against Granite Bay Credit Union.

11. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

13. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

14. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

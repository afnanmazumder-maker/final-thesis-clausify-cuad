FACILITIES ACCESS AND SERVICES AGREEMENT



This Facilities Access And Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Brightwater Campus Services, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Brightwater"), and Ellery Institute of Technology, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Ellery"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Brightwater Campus Services owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Ellery Institute of Technology desires to obtain, and Brightwater Campus Services is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

2. EXPIRATION. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, this Agreement shall expire on 31 December 2027.

3. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

4. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Oregon, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

5. INSURANCE. Ellery Institute of Technology shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Brightwater Campus Services as an additional insured.

6. SURVIVAL. The provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to confidentiality, indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property and governing law, shall so survive.

7. WARRANTY. Brightwater Campus Services warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

8. GRANT OF LICENSE. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Brightwater Campus Services grants to Ellery Institute of Technology a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Software in object code form solely for Ellery Institute of Technology's internal business purposes during the Term.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10. THIRD PARTY BENEFICIARY. The Parties agree that Centurion Assurance Group shall be an intended third party beneficiary of the warranties and indemnities set out in Sections 8 and 9, and shall be entitled to enforce the same directly against Ellery Institute of Technology.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

12. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.

13. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

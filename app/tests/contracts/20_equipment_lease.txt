EQUIPMENT LEASE AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT



This Equipment Lease And Maintenance Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Steelhaven Leasing Corp., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Steelhaven"), and Nordvik Logistics AB, with offices at 1200 Commerce Street ("Nordvik"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Steelhaven Leasing Corp. owns and controls certain products, technology and intellectual property; and

WHEREAS, Nordvik Logistics AB desires to obtain, and Steelhaven Leasing Corp. is willing to grant, certain rights on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

2. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Written notice of non-renewal must be given not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term. If such notice is not timely given, this Agreement shall automatically renew and the Parties shall be bound for the full subsequent term.

3. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

4. INSURANCE. Nordvik Logistics AB shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Steelhaven Leasing Corp. as an additional insured.

5. ASSIGNMENT. Nordvik Logistics AB shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Steelhaven Leasing Corp., and any purported assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

7. CHANGE OF CONTROL. In the event of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets or voting securities of Nordvik Logistics AB, or any transaction resulting in a change in control of Nordvik Logistics AB, Steelhaven Leasing Corp. may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

8. WARRANTY. Steelhaven Leasing Corp. warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

9. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Steelhaven Leasing Corp. may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Nordvik Logistics AB, without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

11. TRANSITION SERVICES. Following termination or expiration of this Agreement, Nordvik Logistics AB shall, at Steelhaven Leasing Corp.'s request, continue to provide the Services for a transition period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days on the terms then in effect, and shall cooperate in the orderly migration of data and accounts.

12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

13. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

14. EXPIRATION. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, this Agreement shall expire on 31 December 2027.

15. FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, or failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

16. PAYMENT TERMS. All invoices are payable in United States dollars within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. All fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be the responsibility of the paying Party.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

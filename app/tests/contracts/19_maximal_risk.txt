STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AND EXCLUSIVE SUPPLY AGREEMENT



This Strategic Alliance And Exclusive Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Vortex Industrial Partners, with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Vortex"), and Ashgrove Systems Incorporated, residing in the State of Delaware ("Incorporated"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Vortex Industrial Partners wishes to employ Incorporated in an executive capacity, and Incorporated wishes to accept such employment, on the terms and conditions set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. NON-COMPETITION. For a period of three (3) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, Ashgrove Systems Incorporated shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as owner, partner, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage in, invest in, or render services to any business that competes with the products or services of Vortex Industrial Partners within the Territory.

2. EXCLUSIVITY. During the Term, Ashgrove Systems Incorporated shall purchase all of its requirements for products of the type covered by this Agreement exclusively from Vortex Industrial Partners, and shall not market, distribute, promote or represent any competing product line from any other source without the prior written consent of Vortex Industrial Partners.

3. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.

4. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Ashgrove Systems Incorporated shall purchase a minimum of twelve thousand (12,000) units during each Contract Year. Any shortfall against the minimum shall be invoiced to Ashgrove Systems Incorporated at the then-current list price and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of invoice, whether or not such units are taken.

5. MOST FAVORED CUSTOMER. Ashgrove Systems Incorporated shall extend to Vortex Industrial Partners pricing, terms and conditions no less favorable than the most favorable pricing, terms and conditions Ashgrove Systems Incorporated extends to any other vendor or customer for comparable goods or services, and shall promptly notify Vortex Industrial Partners if more favorable terms are granted to a third party.

6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

7. ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Ashgrove Systems Incorporated hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to Vortex Industrial Partners all right, title and interest in and to any inventions, improvements, derivative works, modifications, documentation and feedback conceived or developed in connection with this Agreement, together with all patent, copyright, trade secret and moral rights therein, and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

8. PERPETUAL LICENSE. Ashgrove Systems Incorporated grants to Vortex Industrial Partners an irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up license, with the right to sublicense through multiple tiers, to use, reproduce, modify and distribute any Ashgrove Systems Incorporated materials incorporated into or delivered with the Deliverables.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through negotiation between senior executives. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered under the Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, before a single arbitrator.

10. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES. In the event of a breach by Ashgrove Systems Incorporated of its exclusivity or non-competition obligations, Ashgrove Systems Incorporated shall pay to Vortex Industrial Partners liquidated damages in the amount of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence. The Parties agree that this sum is a reasonable estimate of the damages that would be sustained and does not constitute a penalty.

11. UNLIMITED LIABILITY. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Ashgrove Systems Incorporated's liability arising out of its indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or gross negligence shall be unlimited and shall not be subject to any cap, exclusion or limitation set out elsewhere herein.

12. FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, or failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

13. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

14. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until expiration or termination as provided herein.

15. NOTICES. All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic mail, or three (3) business days after being sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the preamble or such other address as a Party may designate in writing.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.

SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT



This Software-As-A-Service Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of 15 January 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Quillstone Cloud, Inc., with its principal place of business at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 1200 ("Quillstone"), and Harborfield Mutual Insurance, residing in the State of Washington ("Insurance"). Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".



RECITALS

WHEREAS, Quillstone Cloud, Inc. wishes to employ Insurance in an executive capacity, and Insurance wishes to accept such employment, on the terms and conditions set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1. GRANT OF LICENSE. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Quillstone Cloud, Inc. grants to Harborfield Mutual Insurance a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Software in object code form solely for Harborfield Mutual Insurance's internal business purposes during the Term.

2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except as expressly provided otherwise, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party shall hold in confidence all Confidential Information of the other Party, shall use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to those of its employees and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out herein. These obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 12.

5. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Written notice of non-renewal must be given not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term. If such notice is not timely given, this Agreement shall automatically renew and the Parties shall be bound for the full subsequent term.

6. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

7. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Quillstone Cloud, Inc. may terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Harborfield Mutual Insurance, without liability other than payment for services properly rendered prior to the effective date of termination.

8. AUDIT. Quillstone Cloud, Inc. may, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, inspect and audit the books, records and facilities of Harborfield Mutual Insurance relating to its performance under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%), Harborfield Mutual Insurance shall bear the cost of the audit.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with all Schedules and Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, proposals and representations, whether written or oral. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10. WARRANTY. Quillstone Cloud, Inc. warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with the Documentation, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

11. INSURANCE. Harborfield Mutual Insurance shall maintain, at its own expense and throughout the Term, commercial general liability insurance of not less than two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per occurrence, together with professional liability and workers' compensation coverage as required by law, and shall name Quillstone Cloud, Inc. as an additional insured.

12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS. The relationship of the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

13. ASSIGNMENT. Harborfield Mutual Insurance shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Quillstone Cloud, Inc., and any purported assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

14. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located therein.

15. FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes, or failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

16. EXPIRATION. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, this Agreement shall expire on 31 December 2027.

17. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances in the performance of its obligations hereunder, including without limitation those relating to anti-corruption, export control, data protection and employment.



IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.
